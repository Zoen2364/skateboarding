What is Skateboarding?

Skateboarding is a popular sport and recreational activity where a person rides and performs movements on a skateboard. It combines balance, coordination, creativity and practice.

Why Skateboarding?

  • Improves balance
  • Develops coordination
  • Builds confidence
  • Provides physical exercise
  • Encourages creativity

Parts of a Skateboard

Deck

The deck is the main wooden or composite board that you stand on.

Trucks

Trucks connect the wheels to the deck and help the skateboard turn.

Wheels

Wheels allow the skateboard to move across the ground.

Bearings

Bearings are placed inside the wheels and help them spin smoothly.

Grip Tape

Grip tape is placed on top of the deck to help your feet stay in position.

Bolts

Bolts attach the trucks securely to the deck.

Beginner's Guide

1. Find Your Stance

Your stance is the position of your feet on the skateboard. The two common stances are Regular and Goofy.

2. Learn to Balance

Start on a stable, flat surface and practice standing comfortably on the board.

3. Learn to Push

Place your front foot on the board and gently push with your other foot.

4. Learn to Turn

Practice making gentle turns while keeping your balance.

5. Practice Stopping

Learn how to slow down and stop safely before trying tricks.

Skateboarding Safety

Always put safety first while learning.

  • 🪖 Wear a helmet.
  • 🛡️ Use knee and elbow protection.
  • 👟 Wear suitable shoes.
  • 🛣️ Practice in a safe area.
  • 👀 Be aware of people and obstacles.
  • 🏃 Learn basic balance before attempting tricks.
